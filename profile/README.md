# Welcome to Shrenio

At **EVERYTHING LTD**, we build reliable, scalable software that automates everyday operations across education, hospitality, and commerce. From managing institutions to integrating real-time AI vision, we focus on practical technology that works seamlessly at scale.

> Most of our core products are currently developed in private repositories as we prepare for upcoming releases.

---

### What we are building

| Product Area | Overview |
| :--- | :--- |
| **School Management System** | End-to-end platform for managing admissions, attendance, academics, staff operations, and parent communication. |
| **Payment System** | Fast, secure billing, fee collection, invoicing, and automated payment reconciliation built for institutions and businesses. |
| **Hotel & Restaurant Management (with AI Vision)** | Complete hospitality operations suite—covering bookings, POS, and inventory—enhanced with **AI Camera Detection** for smart monitoring, occupancy tracking, and operational analytics. |

---

### Why EVERYTHING
- **Unified ecosystem:** Operations, billing, and analytics designed to work together out of the box.
- **Practical AI:** Computer vision and automation applied where they save real time and reduce manual errors.
- **Built for scale:** Clean architecture focused on reliability, security, and everyday usability.
